Education
======

- **PhD in Computational Geomechanics**, Imperial College London, 2018–2023  
  Thesis: *Seismic performance of tailings sand dams*. Supervisors: Dr Stavroula Kontoe and Prof Lidija Zdravković.  
  I studied the static and seismic response of tailings sand dams with advanced finite element analyses, modelling tailings behaviour within the Critical State Soil Mechanics framework. The constitutive models were calibrated against monotonic and cyclic laboratory data from copper mines.
- **MSc in Soil Mechanics and Engineering Seismology**, Imperial College London, 2016–2017  
  Dissertation: *Canyon topography effects on ground motion*. Supervisor: Dr Stavroula Kontoe.
- **MSc in Geotechnical Engineering**, University of Chile, 2008–2010  
  Thesis: *Monotonic and cyclic triaxial equipment at high pressures and its application in tailings sands* (in Spanish). Supervisor: Dr Ramón Verdugo.  
  I designed and built high-pressure triaxial equipment for monotonic and cyclic loading at confining pressures of up to 6 MPa.
- **Civil Engineer (BSc)**, University of Chile, 2002–2008
