Since 2008 I have worked on geotechnical characterisation and advanced numerical modelling for mining and civil infrastructure projects in Chile, Peru and Mexico. Selected projects are listed below.

Tailings storage facilities and mine waste
------

- **2025** – Collahuasi FEL2 Growth Project, waste dumps (Chile): geotechnical support and expert advice.
- **2025** – Quebrada Blanca Phase 2, Engineer of Record services (Chile): geotechnical consultant for physical stability, numerical modelling and geotechnical characterisation campaigns for tailings sands.
- **2024–2025** – Sierra Gorda, Engineer of Record services (Chile): geotechnical consultant for physical stability through numerical modelling.
- **2025** – Las Tórtolas Dam life extension engineering (Chile): geotechnical consultant.
- **2025** – Las Tórtolas Hydraulic Dewatered Stacking (HDS), pre-feasibility study (Chile): geotechnical consultant.
- **2025** – Cozamin, Engineer of Record services (Mexico): geotechnical consultant for physical stability through numerical modelling.
- **2025** – Los Leones TSF, Codelco Andina Division (DAND), Engineer of Record services (Chile): geotechnical consultant for physical stability through numerical modelling.
- **2024–2025** – Spence TSF (Chile), client BHP – Minera Spence S.A.: geotechnical consultant for numerical modelling.
- **2024** – El Torito Dam (Chile), client Anglo American Chile: numerical model for the 235 Mt condition (crest elevation 413 m a.s.l.).
- **2018** – La Brea tailings storage, Caserones (Chile): 3D numerical model.
- **2017** – Talabre tailings storage, Codelco (Chile): 2D numerical model.
- **2016** – Quebrada Honda TSF growth (Peru), client Southern Peru: geotechnical leader for the detailed engineering project, including a 2D numerical model.
- **2014** – Quebrada Honda (Peru), Southern Peru: geotechnical characterisation of tailings sands.
- **2014** – Zaldívar tailings storage, AMSA (Chile): 2D numerical model.
- **2013** – San Esteban tailings storage, Lundin Mining (Chile): 2D numerical model.
- **2012** – El Salvador, Codelco (Chile): geotechnical characterisation of leaching residues.
- **2008, 2011** – Quellaveco tailings storage (Peru): 3D numerical models.
- **2008** – Caserones sand stockpile (Chile): 3D numerical model.
- **2008** – Los Leones tailings storage, Codelco (Chile): 3D numerical model.

Civil infrastructure
------

- **2015–2016** – Santiago Metro, extension of Lines 3 and 6 (Chile), client Metro de Santiago: geotechnical leader for site investigation, geotechnical characterisation and advanced numerical modelling of new and existing stations.
- **2015–2016** – Small dams in central Chile, client Department of Hydraulic Works (DOH): geotechnical leader for characterisation and design.
- **2016** – Los Ángeles water reservoir, DOH (Chile): 2D numerical model.
- **2011** – ELT Cerro Armazones Observatory, ESO (Chile): 3D numerical model of topographic amplification.
- **2011** – Social housing (Chile), study for SERVIU: geotechnical vulnerability of seven housing complexes affected by the 2010 Maule earthquake.
- **2011** – Huella Tres Puntas, Tocopilla (Chile): slope stability study of an area with severe collapses during the 2007 earthquake.
- **2010–2011** – Alto Río Building, Concepción (Chile): geotechnical study of the building's collapse during the 2010 earthquake.
